Is India rethinking its short-term military recruitment scheme?

The Indian government recently changed its short-term military recruitment policy, allowing young soldiers to serve longer and get another chance at a permanent military career. This shift comes after feedback and protests from youth who want more stability and growth opportunities in defence services.

Why it matters

Defence jobs are a popular career choice for many Indian youth, so changes in recruitment policy can directly impact opportunities after school or college. Understanding these shifts helps teenagers make informed choices about their future.

300 Elephants, One Village, An Extraordinary Idea

Once a hotspot for human-elephant conflict, Assam's Hatikhuli village radically changed its approach by planting food for elephants and creating safe corridors. Led by a 90-year-old conservationist, the villagers now peacefully coexist with over 300 elephants that migrate through their land every year.

Why it matters

This innovative and compassionate conservation effort teaches teenagers how communities can turn conflict into coexistence. It offers a model for dealing with wildlife and environmental issues positively.
