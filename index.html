300 Elephants, One Village, An Extraordinary Idea

In Assam’s Hatikhuli village, people and elephants once clashed—but villagers, led by conservationists, found ways for both to coexist by planting food for elephants and creating safe migration corridors. Now, over 300 elephants pass through safely every year, proving that communities can find solutions for people-wildlife conflict.

Why it matters

This inspiring story of peaceful coexistence between humans and wildlife can motivate teenagers to think creatively about solving environmental problems and shows the impact one community can make.
