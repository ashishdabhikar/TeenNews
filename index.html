Is India rethinking its short-term military recruitment scheme?

India has adjusted its military recruitment policy, allowing young soldiers a longer initial stint and giving them a better opportunity to transition into permanent service. This comes after criticism of the previous short-term policy that many felt was too restrictive for career development.

Why it matters

For students considering a future in the armed forces, these changes could open new opportunities for stable careers and national service, while also impacting youth employment and defence readiness.
